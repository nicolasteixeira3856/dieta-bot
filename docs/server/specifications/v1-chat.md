# Specification — POST /v1/chat and POST /v1/close

## Context and goal

Chat sends a profile, today's snapshot, up to 12 messages and an optional photo. The server returns prose, a structured estimate and a suggested slot. It is stateless and never records the day.

## Scope

- `POST /v1/chat`, authenticated JSON; normal turns and `compact=true` digests.
- `POST /v1/close`, authenticated JSON: the day and week closure text (rule 17).
- Request, photo, timeout and error boundaries: [HTTP contract](../../api-contract.md).
- The client does not call estimate/fit from Chat ([product Chat](../../produto/specifications/chat.md), rule 13).
- Optional installation correlation: [identity/audit](../../content-policy/specifications/identity-and-audit.md#closed-test-profile).
- Content handling: [content policy](../../content-policy/specifications/content-policy.md) and rules 12–16 below.

## Out of scope

Photo, user, day and memory persistence; streaming; multipart; other models; server-side ceiling calculation; the [production audit gate](../../content-policy/production-gate.md).

## Functional rules

1. Authentication matches estimate: invalid or missing `X-Invite` returns 401.
2. Model: `gpt-6-luna`, `store=false`. The Chat generation runs at `reasoning.effort=low` ([ADR-054](../adrs/ADR-054-chat-reasoning-effort-low.md)); compaction, closures, estimate and fit run at `none`. The effort is a server constant, never a client field; the 60-second deadline of rule 16 covers the measured p95 (16 s).
3. Each turn has fixed instructions followed by `PROFILE`, `MEMORY`, `RECENT`, `RECENT_DAYS`, `COPY_SOURCE`, `DAY` (with `local_time`), `DIGESTS`, `HISTORY` (up to 12 messages), `PENDING_ADDITION`, `WINDOWS`/`BUDGET`, current text and optional image; the blocks stable within a day come before DAY so the provider's prefix cache covers them between turns, and an empty block is omitted. Instructions are fixed within each (meal-change capability branch, `profile.tone`) pair for prompt caching: four Chat prefixes, whose last rules are PLANNED, FORMAT and TONE (rules 3i–3k). They are assembled only from the declared registry in `server/chat_instructions.py`: owned rules, reviewed synthetic examples, a pt-BR cue lexicon and one reference-portions rule: about twenty staple foods with their energy and macros per 100 g from the Tabela Brasileira de Composição de Alimentos (TACO, 4th edition; milk rows from the IBGE table), generic data recorded with its provenance ([ADR-041](../../produto/adrs/ADR-041-reference-portions-in-chat-instructions.md)). Foods, brands, nutrients, habits, meal names, times and ids of a person reach the model only through the per-request context below, never through the fixed instructions; example and cue provenance follows [ADR-033](../../content-policy/adrs/ADR-033-global-chat-example-provenance.md). The cue lexicon and the contracted reply copy are pt-BR: a release in another language or market requires replacing them first. Effective meal-change requests additionally serialize PENDING_ADDITION before the current user message; it is untrusted proposal context, never a DAY record. `DAY` carries recorded slot text and kcal/P/C/G (a planned slot as `{id}:planned ({kcal}kcal, {p}P {c}C {g}G, text="...")`, rule 3k), plus `remaining_kcal` when supplied and, with it, `remaining_p`, `remaining_c`, `remaining_g` (target − eaten, whole numbers, may be negative, computed by the server; [ADR-043](../../produto/adrs/ADR-043-plan-objective-protein-and-meal-window.md)). With `remaining_kcal` the request also ends with per-request `WINDOWS` (when at least two upcoming empty meals remain: each upcoming empty meal's share of the remainder and its protein) and `BUDGET` (the window of a plan for each upcoming empty meal, with its reservations, and the protein floor) lines, never in the cached prefix. An empty meal whose time passed more than 90 minutes ago is over: it is neither reserved nor listed. A planned slot appears in `WINDOWS` as `{slot}: planejado {kcal} kcal` and in `BUDGET` with its own window; the empty meals share what the plans leave.
   - Structured memory (`facts` present, even empty): `MEMORY: permanent {n}/30, dynamic {n}/40`. Permanent/dynamic lines: `{id} {category}[ slot={slot}] {key}: {text}[ · {kcal} kcal · P {p} · C {c} · G {g}] (seen {n} days[, last {date}])`; the numbers are printed only when the fact carries all four. The legacy `memory` text is ignored.
   - With `temp_facts: true` and structured memory (v5), append `, temp {n}/5` to that header. A temporary line is `{id} {category} {key}: {text} (temp since {last_seen})`; a missing date is `unknown`. The client supplies creation date in `last_seen`. Without the capability the permanent/dynamic header is unchanged.
   - Legacy clients without `facts` retain `MEMORY: {memory}`. Their `temp_facts` flag is ignored.
   - `RECENT`, when supplied by any client: `{date} {weekday} {slot_id} {slot_name}: "{text}" {kcal}kcal {p}P {c}C {g}G`; a null slot is `Outros`.
   - `RECENT_DAYS`, when `recent_days` is supplied: one line per day in date order, `{date} {weekday}: {kcal} kcal de {ceiling} · P {p} · C {c} · G {g} · registrado[ · passou em: {slot name}] · sem registro em: {slot names or —}`, or `{date} {weekday}: sem registro` for a day with nothing recorded. The model copies these numbers and never sums or averages them.
   - `COPY_SOURCE` (rule 3d): the RECENT rows of a named-day request, resolved in code from the current message, one block per clause that names a day: `COPY_SOURCE:` and one RECENT line, or `COPY_SOURCE: ambiguous` and every candidate line.
   - An assistant history message may end with `[refeição sugerida: {slot name}]`. It is the assistant's suggestion, never the user's words. Client creation of this marker belongs to A38.
3a. Intent is `log`, `plan`, `question` or `skip`. `log`: ate/is eating, answers a question about that meal, or names food alone without a verb or question. `plan`: will eat, asks quantities, a recipe or whether food fits, or asks openly what to eat, order or make, how to organise a meal out, or says they have no idea what to eat (ADR-041). `question`: a greeting, app question, food nutrition question or memory statement without food to estimate. `skip`: a meal did not happen today (`pulei o café`, `hoje não almocei`, without `ainda`), or a firm decision it will not happen (`hoje não vou jantar`). A pending meal (`ainda não almocei`) or hedged skip (`acho que não vou jantar`) is `question`, or `plan` when asking what to eat, never `skip`. `skip` is a message that only skips. Skips are also a fact of any turn ([ADR-047](../../produto/adrs/ADR-047-skips-alongside-other-actions.md)): the model lists in `skip_slots` every profile slot whose name matches a meal the user says did not happen today or firmly will not, next to any intent (a skip and a meal, a skip and a plan or question, several skips); a pending or hedged meal, a meal with no profile slot of that name, an empty earlier slot and the slot of the meal being estimated are never listed. Estimate is present for log/plan and null for question/skip. When uncertain, past eating is log; future/conditional or quantities are plan. Off-topic requests are handled under rule 12. Clients before v4 receive skip as question with the same reply.
3b. `meal_text` is the entire meal, including corrections, in pt-BR, within the Unicode limits of the [HTTP contract](../../api-contract.md#meal-change-capability), foods and quantities only. Never the latest answer alone or commentary. Descriptions within their bound remain complete; overflow fails safely without a record or memory mutation, for every client.
3c. A plan's reply gives grams per item and totals as `kcal · P · C · G`. Plans assume rather than ask about the food; the reply states assumptions, and estimate.question is null. An open request names one concrete dish or order (at most two alternatives) with grams and total; the reply never gives behavioural advice (eating speed, water, satiety) and never defers to an external source such as the delivery app or the restaurant; a named venue or occasion gets what that venue typically serves (ADR-041). For every client:
   - Cooking help ([ADR-039](../../produto/adrs/ADR-039-plan-cooking-and-budget-choice.md)): a request for a recipe or for what to make names a real dish with the user's foods as the base. The model may add up to three common, low-cost foods chosen for flavor, volume, protein or satiety for few kcal, each marked `(opcional)` in the reply and included in the items and totals. The reply lists ingredients with grams and up to five numbered steps with temperature and time. A plan whose message names an appliance or a way of preparing a dish the user will make is a cooking plan too, with steps for that dish or for the chosen option of a plan with options ([ADR-056](../../produto/adrs/ADR-056-plan-decision-line-and-option-budget.md)); other plans have no preparation steps.
   - No budget arithmetic in the reply: it never says whether the dish fits, what is left or the excess, except in the decision line of rule 3n whose numbers the server writes, and never computes the day's totals. When the user leaves the dish to the model it is built to fit `remaining_kcal` when possible; a dish with stated foods or amounts is not shrunk for the budget.
   - The model reports `plan_budget` `{reserved, choice}` for a plan with an estimate, null otherwise. `reserved` lists each other meal still to be eaten today that the message or MEMORY states, as a short pt-BR label and its stated or estimated kcal; such a meal never enters items, meal_text or totals. `choice` is `over_ok` when the user said going over is fine, `fit` when the dish must fit, otherwise null, read from the current message and earlier turns about the dish. The model never asks about the budget.
   - With a `BUDGET_TARGET` line at the end of the input (rule 5f), the model rebuilds the same dish at or below that number, shrinking calorie-dense foods first and keeping the additions where possible.
3d. A named-day request (an equality word such as `mesmo`, `igual`, `o de`, `repeti`, a day word `ontem`, `anteontem`, a weekday or `semana passada`, and a slot named or implied by the eating verb, in one clause of the message) is resolved by the server before the model runs: the RECENT rows of that slot on that date become the `COPY_SOURCE` block of rule 3. With one row the model copies its foods, kcal and macros exactly, confidence high, no question, and begins the reply with the slot name and the row's weekday; with `ambiguous` it asks which row, naming the dates, with estimate null; with no block (no row) it asks what was eaten with estimate null and never picks a RECENT row itself. Habitual words never produce a block. `O de sempre` / `o mesmo de sempre` first uses a routine fact of that slot, copying the kcal and macros on its line when present (a routine without numbers is estimated from the reference portions, confidence medium). Otherwise compare the latest record of that slot on each of the two most recent distinct days (last record within a day). Foods and quantities must match; a different brand of the same food is allowed, changed amounts or added/missing foods are not. A bare habitual-meal report is log. On a match, return the estimate, copy the most recent foods and numbers and name the copied weekday in reply without asking whether the user ate it. Otherwise, including fewer than two days, ask what was eaten with estimate null.
3e. Use memory that resolves uncertainty, without asking again, and cite its id in `memory_used`; a fact used to avoid, contrast or vary from a habit (a plan different from the usual) is cited too. Explicit habits propose permanent add/replace; explicit forgetting proposes remove; brand/type/portion in a log reinforces or adds a dynamic fact; matching routines reinforce; a new habit can add a dynamic routine. A routine add or replace carries the meal's kcal, p, c and g; every other proposal carries null for them. Permanent/dynamic keys match only that family. Never save one-off meals, day totals, health conditions or one-off labels as habits. At permanent capacity, ask which least-seen fact to forget before adding.
   - Only with temp capacity in MEMORY, a specific product's supplied label/nutrition values or a dish estimated for later proposes `add`, kind `temp`, category `portion`, slot null. Keep the product, serving basis and given numbers even with incomplete nutrients; never invent missing values in the fact. The proposal is independent of the reply/estimate, including plans for another day. Generic calorie questions, habits/preferences and the meal being logged do not create temporaries.
   - A matching T reference supplies its numbers on every use, including later portions, and its id goes into `memory_used`. Use never reinforces, promotes or removes it. An explicit request to forget can remove it. Changed reference data replaces an existing T id; a temp key never replaces a permanent/dynamic fact. Storage, expiry and capacity belong to the client under [ADR-029](../../produto/adrs/ADR-029-fatos-temporarios-compactacao.md).
3f. Reply never claims a meal was recorded or skipped: the app writes it and shows a receipt ([ADR-028](../../produto/adrs/ADR-028-registro-autonomo.md)). Applies to every client. Each listed skip gets one short neutral clause in the reply (`{slot} de hoje fora.`); a skip of a meal with no profile slot gets one clause saying no meal with that name exists today and that its Home card can be held to skip it (ADR-047).
3g. Internal record marks apply to every model request and drive v4 responses. `record_intent: clear` means stated eating/skipping, explicit registration, a meal name followed by food, a plate photo without text or with stated eating, an answer to a meal question, a concrete portion report referring to previously discussed/estimated food, or `Pode estimar assim`. Food alone, hypotheticals and food photos with a nutrition question are `unsure`; the latter still have log/estimate. Plan/question are unsure. `today` uses DAY.date at the supplied local_time for the whole dialogue, including undated eating statements in history and their answers. Past tense alone is not yesterday. `meal_day: other` means food was **eaten** on another day, not merely asked about, estimated, bought, cooked or planned then. `O mesmo de ontem` is today's meal. Before 05:00, an undated dinner is today only if that profile slot is scheduled before 05:00; otherwise it is last night's dinner, other. Other undated meals and food eaten `agora` are today. Skip replies are one short neutral line without advice.
3h. The assistant is Tali, the meal-tracking assistant of the Fibrai app ([ADR-035](../../produto/adrs/ADR-035-tali-in-app-identity.md)). It uses the name only when the user asks who it is or what the app is, in one short line; no persona and no greeting beyond the other rules. The scope rule of every route names the product Fibrai.
3i. Tone ([ADR-044](../../produto/adrs/ADR-044-assistant-tone-and-closures.md)): `profile.tone` picks the TONE rule, last in the prefix. Scope, refusals and `safety_support` (rule 12) are decided first and never change with the tone. `seco`: numbers first, no judgment, no advice beyond the dish, no praise. `duro`: after the answer of an in-scope log or plan, at most two short lines of direct critique from DAY, BUDGET, RECENT_DAYS and RECENT (the meal that broke the ceiling or its window, the protein still missing, the dinner or weekend pattern named with the weekday words of the RECENT_DAYS lines marked `registrado`, once a day: not when an assistant turn of today in HISTORY already names it ([ADR-056](../../produto/adrs/ADR-056-plan-decision-line-and-option-budget.md)); a day `sem registro` or with meals `sem registro em` is never called a day below the ceiling), one practical adjustment, and, when the log differs from a plan agreed earlier today, the difference in kcal and what to change in the open meals; no slogans, praise or softening; question-only and skip replies carry no critique. Both tones: never mention body, weight or appearance (not even to decline the subject), never suggest eating below the ceiling, skipping a meal, fasting or compensating.
3j. Formatting ([ADR-045](../../produto/adrs/ADR-045-rich-replies-in-chat-bubbles.md)): the FORMAT rule names the reply subset of the [contract](../../api-contract.md#post-v1chat): bold only on the deciding numbers, the dish name and a one-word verdict (in a log only the meal total), `- ` lines for options, ingredients and foods to avoid, numbered steps only in a recipe, one `| Item | Gramas |` table of at most six rows that a recipe always uses for its ingredients; no other markup. Question-only replies, skip replies, the other-day notice and the closing lines carry no markers. The server keeps the reply inside the subset (rule 5g).
3k. Planned slot ([ADR-046](../../produto/adrs/ADR-046-planned-meal-reservation.md)): a DAY slot with `status: planned` carries the plan the user reserved in the app; nothing of it was eaten. A log into it is a new meal of that slot (`operation: new`, `base_slot` null, not occupied for ADR-032) whose estimate is what was eaten. The model never writes the difference to the plan: after a released log into a planned slot of today the server ends the prose with `+{n} kcal sobre o plano.`, `−{n} kcal abaixo do plano.` or `Igual ao plano.` (server total of rule 4a minus the plan kcal), replacing any difference the model wrote; under `duro` one adjustment for the open meals may precede it. A plan for a planned slot is answered as a plan and replaces the reservation only if the user reserves again. The model never says a meal was reserved, locked or kept. A planned total is not a copied record (rule 4a keep).
3l. Workout ([ADR-049](../../produto/adrs/ADR-049-workout-energy-via-chat.md)): a report of a workout done today, with or without kcal, is in scope; training plans, exercise prescriptions and physiology are out of scope (rule 12 and the [content policy](../../content-policy/specifications/content-policy.md)). The WORKOUT rule fills `workout {kcal, mode}` only for a stated number of kcal (`replace` by default, `add` for more kcal or a second workout over `DAY.workout_kcal`), never inferred from duration, distance, heart rate, pace or the kind of exercise; a workout without a number is intent question with a one-line request for the kcal shown by the watch or app. A workout next to a meal or a plan keeps both, and every estimate uses DAY as sent. The reply is one short line with the number and, when `eat_back` is not zero, that the app recomputes the credit; never a credit, a ceiling or "recorded"; under `duro` a workout alone gets no critique. Shaping keeps an integer kcal 1–5000 and a known mode, else null; only the effective `workout` capability returns the field (record `auto` for a workout-only turn, rule 5d).
3m. Typed actions ([ADR-050](../../produto/adrs/ADR-050-typed-actions-per-message.md)): the meal-change branch answers with the strict schema `chat_turn_actions` `{reply, scope, actions, memory_updates, memory_used, digest}`; each action `{id, type, slot, estimate, record_intent, meal_day, meal_change, workout, recipe_id, options, plan_budget}`, `type` `log`, `plan`, `skip`, `workout`, `recipe_recall` or `question` (`recipe_id` and `options` null). Its prefix replaces INTENT and SKIPS with ACTIONS: one action per thing the message does, logs, plans, workouts and recalls in the order stated and skips last in profile order, a slot both eaten and skipped is a log, at most six (the reply names the rest), a question never next to another action (a clarification goes into that action's `estimate.question`), a plan after a log sized with the log eaten, every rule that names `intent`, `estimate`, `skip_slots` or `meal_change` read as the field of the action, the ADR-047 skip clauses (`{slot} de hoje fora.`, the no-slot clause). The server normalizes the list (`server/actions.py`; a single-estimate payload is read as its actions), regenerates once when a question sits next to another action and otherwise drops the question actions, then shapes each action as a single estimate, in order, with the reply threaded through: server total, add/revise validation and copy, clarify and record gates, plan difference, meal window and plan budget, protein boost; each action sees DAY after the released logs of today before it. A held action keeps the shared reply. The closing lines run once, after the last released log or plan of today, with the meals the message skips or holds counted as skipped; a closing line for a skipped or held meal is removed. The response view is in the [contract](../../api-contract.md#actions-capability). The legacy branch (no `meal_changes`) keeps rules 3a and 3f as written.
3n. Plan options and discovery ([ADR-051](../../produto/adrs/ADR-051-plan-option-identity-and-chat-discovery.md)): an open request is a plan with two options, each with an id (`o1`, `o2`), a short name and its own estimate, presented in the reply as `**Opção 1: {name}**` and `**Opção 2: {name}**` with grams, total and protein; the action's estimate is option 1 and, in the meal-change branch, its `options` holds both (shaping keeps ids `o1`/`o2`, unique, a name of at most 60 characters and an estimate with energy; each option total is the server total of rule 4a); an open request gets no protein boost. A comparison ([ADR-056](../../produto/adrs/ADR-056-plan-decision-line-and-option-budget.md)), two alternative dishes the user names with a choice question (the cue lexicon lists the markers), is a plan with options too, each option the user's dish as named, with no leaner/indulgent pair and no boost. The reply of a plan with options opens with the decision line `Vai de {name}: ~{kcal} kcal · P {p} g, cabe na janela do {meal}. {other} passa ~{n} kcal.` (`{other} também cabe.` when both fit; `…, passa ~{n} kcal da janela do {meal}; {other} passa ~{m}.` when both pass; `…, passa ~{n} kcal da janela do {meal}. {other} cabe.` when only the other fits), then the options; the option the line names, matched by name (`Opção {n}`, the same name, one name inside the other, else the one sharing the most words), is the chosen option and becomes the action's estimate; without a line naming one, the estimate stays option 1. Components the message leaves open are completed from RECENT records of a dish of the same kind before a generic assumption, each assumption said once. A later reference to an option by number or name (the cue lexicon lists the markers) is answered about that option only: a question quotes its grams, eating it is a log copying it unchanged. The compaction keeps, for the last turn with numbered options, `Opção {n}: {name}, {kcal} kcal, P {protein} g` (the one exception to rule 7's no-numbers rule). With `discovery: true` the input ends with `DISCOVERY: first_open` before the message: a greeting gets one short line and up to four skippable questions as `- ` lines (the FORMAT exception), the answers propose declared routines (`declared: true`, numbers from the reference portions), permanent preferences and `equipment` facts, with nothing logged; a skip proposes nothing. Memory categories `equipment` (an appliance, permanent) and `liked` (a dish the user approved after eating it, dynamic, slot of that meal, its numbers) exist in the schema and the MEMORY CHANGES rule; plans prefer a declared appliance and a liked dish; a restated preference is a replace, a branded routine log a reinforce.
3o. Saved recipes ([ADR-052](../../produto/adrs/ADR-052-saved-recipes.md)): `recipes` is serialized as `RECIPES` (`{id} {name} · {kcal} kcal · P · C · G · {key foods}`) and `recipe_full` as `RECIPE_FULL` (header, `ingredientes:` with grams and kcal, `passos:`), after `RECENT_DAYS` and before `COPY_SOURCE`. The RECIPES rule (meal-change branch only): a request to see a saved recipe is a `recipe_recall` with the matching id and one short line; two matches ask which one with a null id; none says no saved recipe has that name and offers to build one, never inventing it; eating a saved recipe with no stated change is a log copying it (confidence high), with a stated change a log re-estimated from `RECIPE_FULL` (confidence medium), adapting it for today a plan rebuilt from `RECIPE_FULL` under PROTEIN and WINDOW, each with `recipe_id`; a cooking plan with steps fills `recipe {name, ingredients, steps}`; a request to save is answered that the app saves it from the button; approval of a saved recipe proposes a `liked` fact. The schema enumerates the request's recipe ids; shaping keeps a `recipe_id` only when it is in `recipes`, bounds `recipe`, and a log of a saved recipe at confidence high takes the recipe's totals (from `recipe_full`, else its index line) with no items, as a copied record; recipe totals join the copied-record totals of rule 4a.
4. Read eating day, intent to record and slot separately. A record request/complaint for an identifiable meal is log with clear intent; reconstruct every food and answer from HISTORY/DIGESTS. It does not change the eating day: `registra o almoço de ontem` remains other. An explicit day correction overrides an assistant assumption or digest. `É de hoje` alone after a generic nutrition question does not create a meal log. Without identifiable food, ask what it was. If the current report identifies food and quantity but the earlier estimate is unavailable, make a fresh estimate from that report.
   - Slot priority: current user-named meal; earlier user-named meal being continued; earlier assistant suggestion for that meal; recorded meal being corrected; local profile time or nearest empty slot. Never ask which meal when already named or suggested. Profile names/times are authoritative, including unusual schedules.
   - Distinguish known/usable, omitted and explicitly unavailable details per food and attribute, using current text, history, digests, applicable memory and photo context. Approximate values, counts, sizes and household measures are usable. Omission alone does not mean inability: ask material omitted portion/size and preparation details together unless context resolves them. Draft assumptions do not count as supplied facts. Never repeat a resolved question. A user saying they cannot know, remember or determine a detail closes it even in the first message: do not request it again with another unit, synonym or approximate qualifier. This applies to weight, brand, preparation and ingredients, without erasing other known values or closing unrelated doubts.
   - When weight is unavailable, use counts, supplied size/household measures and visual evidence. A photo supports estimation, not measured weight. A still-material size question or familiar comparison is allowed unless supplied or ruled out. Ask remaining useful doubts together, at most three short questions. After an answer, ask again only about food still lacking any usable portion and materially changing totals; assume sauce, oil, cream, cheese, seasoning and usual coffee/tea amounts. When every food of a first message already has a usable amount, cooking fat, milk type, sugar and usual coffee/tea amounts are likewise assumed with their common value, stated in one short reply line and never asked; a MEMORY fact wins over the common value, and the record mark follows rule 3g (ADR-041, narrowing [ADR-026](../../produto/adrs/ADR-026-perguntas-antes-da-estimativa.md) for that situation). Never cycle back to weight after a size answer.
   - When no material answerable doubt remains, including when alternatives are unavailable, estimate the whole meal with question null. Keep medium/low confidence for assumed portions and state the assumption in one short reply line; never inflate confidence to release the estimate. A later explicit measurement/correction overrides earlier unavailability. Meal-specific inability to answer does not become a memory fact. Preserve every food, count and fractional portion.
   - Resolve additions from eating relationships rather than food category or the next empty slot. Explicit current targets win; an interleaved correction of another meal does not redirect the meal being eaten. Ask when two targets remain plausible. Profile times remain authoritative. Only an unambiguous unnamed addition may use the latest eaten DAY slot.
   - With `meal_changes`, resolve operation and target together. DAY is the sole recorded base. A named occupied target cannot inherit foods from another slot or a digest's old proposed destination. Keep add intent through target answers and repeated quantities. A correction of PENDING_ADDITION replaces the unrecorded delta; a second unrecorded portion joins that delta, while a portion already in DAY is part of the base. A repeated recorded description alone does not imply another portion. Explicit removal/replacement revises the whole meal. `new` is never chosen for an eaten suggested_slot. An answer to the add-or-replace question, even one word, resolves it on the meal that question named: add keeps the named foods and portions as the addition, replace makes them the complete revised meal; neither asks those portions or the meal again.
   - Without the capability, totals still describe the whole meal and item list, including prior foods. Reply separates the addition, previous amount and result instead of calling the addition a replacement. Legacy accounting remains model-estimated; safe delta rerouting requires a capable client.
   - Food eaten on another day may be estimated if requested, but reply states that Chat records only today's meals, including the before-05:00 rule.
4a. A calorie total without food, even for an eaten slot, produces estimate null and a question about food. A meal whose foods all carry grams, millilitres or units is confidence high; medium is for an assumed amount. A canned drink without a stated volume is the most common can of that product in Brazil (350 ml for beer and soda) and the reply states the volume assumed. On a clear log the amount of a side, spread or condiment is assumed and stated, never asked. A question about adding one food to a dish already stated is answered with that food alone (grams, kcal, main macro, one line), never the dish total, and that one-food plan under 150 kcal receives no protein boost (rule 5f). With `meal_changes`, a food already in the recorded text of that meal that the user says they forgot to mention is never an add: a revise with the same foods and totals, or the reaffirmation answer. Nutrients must reflect the food; a food listed in the reference-portions rule (rule 3) takes its energy and macros from that table scaled to the stated amount, deviating only for a stated preparation or brand. When the estimate carries items with energy, the server computes `kcal` as the sum of the item kcal, each rounded once to a whole number (ties upward), and scales `p`, `c` and `g` by the ratio between that sum and the model total, rounded once ([ADR-042](../adrs/ADR-042-estimate-total-is-server-arithmetic.md)); items with a nonfinite or negative kcal or nonpositive grams do not count, and an estimate without usable item energy is unchanged. A model total equal to the kcal of a supplied RECENT row or eaten DAY slot is a copied record (rule 3d, corrections) and keeps the record's numbers. A total the reply quoted in the form `N kcal` is rewritten to the server total. Energy may include sources beyond P/C/G, including alcohol. Never fabricate macros to enforce 4P + 4C + 9G = kcal. Unable to estimate means null, not zeros.
5. Model output uses strict JSON schema `chat_turn`, all properties required with explicit nulls: reply; intent; estimate `{kcal,p,c,g,confidence,question,items,suggested_slot,meal_text}` or null; internal record_intent/meal_day; skip_slots (an array of the per-request slot ids, empty when the profile has none); memory_updates (each with `kcal`, `p`, `c`, `g`, numbers or null); memory_used; digest (null for chat); internal plan_budget (object or null, rule 3c), the same shape for every client; memory updates with `declared` and the categories of rule 3n; workout (`{kcal, mode}` or null, rule 3l), the same shape for every client; and internal scope. The opted-in schema also requires meal_change (object or null), with profile-bound base ids; the legacy schema has no such field. The response adds the configured model. `memory_updates[].kind` always has `permanent`, `dynamic`, `temp`, even for older clients. Slots and fact ids supply the existing per-request enums; `temp_facts` creates no schema or instruction variant for the same ids. Compact uses the separate digest schema.
5a. The server total of rule 4a is applied to the model payload before the budget check, the record gate and shaping, on the first call and on the adjustment call. Shaping deduces invalid intent from estimate (log if present, otherwise question); drops an estimate on question; nulls plan questions; fills empty legacy meal_text from items and rejects overflow without truncation. Memory updates discard invalid op/kind/category, unknown ids for non-add operations, add with an id, routine without valid slot, and empty key/text; truncate key/text to their input limits and retain at most five. A routine update keeps `kcal`, `p`, `c`, `g` rounded to integers when all four are finite, nonnegative and kcal is above zero; otherwise, and on every other category, the four keys are absent from the response. `memory_used` retains known unique ids, at most ten.
   - Temp updates require the effective v5 capability: add with null id, or replace/remove of a known T id; category portion/preference; slot null. Reinforce, routine and any non-null slot are dropped. Operations on T ids with another kind are dropped. Without capability, all temp proposals are dropped.
5c. With `clarify_rounds` (v3), response gains top-level question and estimate.question is always null. For log with estimate, `clarify_gate` checks in order: force (`released_force`); high confidence or no question (`released_confident`); rounds at least three (`released_cap`); repeated assistant question (`released_repeat`); otherwise hold (`asked`). Release keeps the model confidence and estimate, with both questions null. Hold returns estimate null, top-level question and history reply `Entendi: {meal_text}.` + newline + question (question alone without meal_text). Memory lists pass unchanged. Repeat detection uses sentences ending in `?`, lowercase unaccented alphanumeric tokens excluding a fixed short pt-BR stop list, Jaccard at least 0.6 or set inclusion. Plan/question/null estimate bypass the gate. Fallback/refusal has question null. Without rounds there is no gate or top-level question.
   - A v5 held response additionally carries `question_slot`: the sanitized held estimate's profile slot id or null. It is absent for non-held responses and clients without effective temp capability. Older client response shapes stay unchanged. Android consumption belongs to A38.
5d. With rounds and `auto_record: true` (v4), the record gate adds record (`auto`, `ask`, `none`) and skip_slot. First matching rule wins:
   1. Refusal → none (`none_policy`).
   2. Question/plan, held turn, log without estimate or fallback → none (`none_intent`).
   3. Other eating day → none (`none_other_day`).
   4. Skip with profile slot → auto and that skip_slot (`auto_skip`).
   5. Skip without slot → none, intent question (`none_skip_slot`).
   6. Released log without suggested slot → ask (`ask_no_slot`).
   7. Released log with clear intent → auto (`auto_log`).
   8. Otherwise → ask (`ask_unsure`).
   With the effective `workout` capability (rule 3l), a turn whose shaped workout is present and that has no estimate, no question and is not a skip carries `record: auto` (`auto_workout`).
   Force and photo without text count as clear. Missing/invalid meal_day defaults today; record_intent defaults unsure. Skip_slot is null except on rule 4, where it is the first listed skip in profile order. The client applies its guards before recording. Without v4 capability, record/skip_slot are absent and skip becomes question with the same reply.
   With `skip_slots: true` (requires `meal_changes`), every response also carries `skip_slots`: the model's list reduced to profile ids, in profile order, without repeats, minus the suggested slot of a log estimate (the log wins); `[]` on a refusal, a fallback or an other-day turn. Held, plan and question turns keep their skips. The record mark of rules 1–8 is unchanged.
5e. Effective `meal_changes` requests validate operations before clarification and record gates. Invalid/missing metadata on a log candidate fails with null estimate/change, no record and empty memory lists; no fallback to legacy semantics. `new` requires no occupied target, and a `new` aimed at an eaten target with no base is not a failure: the estimate and change are dropped and the turn becomes the unresolved-operation question `{slot} de hoje já tem registro: {text up to 60 characters} ({kcal} kcal). Somo {draft meal_text up to 160 characters}, ~{draft kcal} kcal, a esse registro ou substituo o registro por isso?` (without a usable draft: `Somo a esse registro ou substituo?`; with no round left, the fixed statement of that path); the draft is in the question because HISTORY carries only the reply, so the answer turn rebuilds the add or the revision from it; a null base_slot on an eaten target is that target for add and revise; an operation with a null estimate is the unresolved question; an addition total is recomputed from its items, and a null addition takes the draft estimate when it is a valid addition, else the model's question, else the turn fails; `revise` requires an eaten base matching suggested_slot and validates the complete replacement, whose total is recomputed from the items under rule 4a instead of refused on a mismatch; for `new` and `revise`, items without usable grams or energy (nonpositive or nonfinite grams, negative or nonfinite kcal) are dropped before validation rather than refused, an estimate left with no usable item keeps its totals with an empty item list, and an estimate whose total equals a supplied RECENT row or eaten DAY slot is a copied record that keeps the record's numbers with an empty item list (its breakdown is unknown); `add` validates only the delta then derives the candidate from the matching DAY aggregate. Exact schema, arithmetic, rounding and bounds belong to the [contract](../../api-contract.md#meal-change-capability).
   - Unknown operation has no estimate and no memory effects. If the model explicitly marks meal_change null but supplies provisional numbers with a question, discard the draft and retain the question; this state cannot become an actionable estimate. A missing field or invalid metadata object still fails safely. Ask through top-level question while rounds remain; on force/cap return a non-actionable explanation. Portion force cannot choose an operation. A known add with only an unknown target may ask with its held draft, even with high nutritional confidence; force/cap/repeat can release that delta with null suggested_slot and record ask.
   - Only released actionable log estimates expose meal_change. Held, plan, skip, other-day, failure and refusal responses carry null. Numeric add/revise copy is composed after validation from the same values as the estimate; base text is preserved verbatim. Output moderation covers the composed reply, description and all addition item names.
5f. Plan budget check ([ADR-039](../../produto/adrs/ADR-039-plan-cooking-and-budget-choice.md), window per [ADR-043](../../produto/adrs/ADR-043-plan-objective-protein-and-meal-window.md)), only for a request with the effective `plan_budget` capability. The limit is the meal window: `remaining_kcal` minus the reservation of every other upcoming empty meal of today, each reserved at its expected kcal (the mean of its RECENT records when it has at least two days, else the ceiling divided by the number of meals of the day) capped at its proportional share of the remainder, so the window never reaches zero while something remains; a meal whose time passed more than 90 minutes ago is not reserved, and neither is a meal the answer's `skip` actions skip ([ADR-056](../../produto/adrs/ADR-056-plan-decision-line-and-option-budget.md)). Before generation, an empty meal of today named within three words after a firm skip marker of the cue lexicon, in a clause without a pending or hedge marker, is left out of the `BUDGET` and `WINDOWS` lines; a phrasing outside the lexicon is counted there, and the numbers after generation follow the skip actions either way. The plan's meal is the model's `suggested_slot`, or the nearest upcoming empty meal when it is missing or names a meal that is over. A model `reserved` entry that names a meal of the profile replaces that meal's reservation only when it is larger, is not a figure the server itself served and its number appears in the user's texts; a meal name that is not an upcoming other meal is dropped; any other label is added as a stated reservation. Before the check, the protein boost of a named dish ([ADR-055](../../produto/adrs/ADR-055-protein-boost-hybrid.md)): the model's PROTEIN BOOST rule adds, to a plan of today whose protein is below 30% of `remaining_p` (`protein_floor` of the BUDGET line) and whose kcal leave at least 60 kcal in the window, one or two common protein foods marked `(opcional)` that suit the dish, each its own item, at the end of `meal_text`, and one reply sentence `Para a proteína (opcional): {g} g de {food}, +{kcal} kcal · P {g} g.`. The server validates it (`protein_boost.validate`): the foods of that sentence, wherever it sits, are matched to items and to a fixed table of fifteen foods (per 100 g, TACO or USDA FoodData Central); a food is dropped when the dish is at or above the floor or the room is under 60 kcal, when the plan is for another day (`meal_day` other, or `amanhã`, `semana que vem` in the message), when it is outside the table, against a diet fact (a permanent preference fact stating vegetarian, vegan or an excluded food or group), already in the dish, a repeat, a third food, or above 150 g or off its kitchen step (50 g for egg, 25 g otherwise); a survivor is shrunk by steps until it fits the room and the protein gap; items, totals (table numbers), `meal_text` and the reply sentence are rewritten to what survives. When nothing survives, or the model proposed nothing, and the boost is due, the server appends one or two foods from its own table (frango desfiado, ovo cozido, patinho moído cozido, skipping a food the dish has or the diet excludes), sized to the room and the gap. A one-food plan under 150 kcal (a food added to a stated dish) is not a dish and gets no boost. The adjustment plan of a `BUDGET_TARGET` call is not boosted. After a log or plan of today with a `WINDOWS` line, the reply ends with one closing line per other upcoming empty meal, `{meal}: {food} ~{kcal} kcal · P {protein}`: the model names the food (its routine or RECENT record when one exists), the server writes the numbers (the other meals share what is left after this one, by expected kcal) and adds the line of a meal the model left out with that meal's latest RECENT food or `a definir`; a closing written inline is moved to its own line, and one for the answered, eaten or skipped meal is dropped. The dish kcal is the server total of rule 4a. The model's `plan_budget` is shaped first: at most three reservations; label trimmed, 1–40 characters; kcal rounded to an integer 1–3000; invalid entries dropped; an unknown choice becomes null. Without the capability the field is stripped and every response shape is unchanged.
   - The check applies to an in-scope `plan` with an estimate above zero, a meal day of today and a non-null `remaining_kcal`. `limit_kcal` is `fit_kcal` when sent, otherwise `remaining_kcal` minus the reservation kcal. `over_kcal` is the estimate kcal minus `limit_kcal`, rounded up, never below zero.
   - Adjusting means `fit_kcal` was sent or the model's choice is `fit`. When adjusting with `limit_kcal` at least 1 and `over_kcal` above zero, the server makes one more model call with the same input plus the last line `BUDGET_TARGET: {limit_kcal} kcal` (per-request context, never fixed instructions). The second result replaces the first only when it is an in-scope plan of today with an estimate above zero; it is then checked against the same target, reservations and choice. A failed, late or invalid second call keeps the first plan. There is no third call: a plan still above the target returns with `over_kcal` above zero.
   - `limit_kcal` below 1 makes no adjustment call and returns the over state.
   - A plan with options is checked on its chosen option (rule 3n), so `plan_budget`, the adjustment and the closing lines use that option; each option gains `over_kcal` = max(0, its kcal − `limit_kcal`, rounded up). The server then rewrites the decision line from `limit_kcal`, the chosen option's meal and the server totals of both options, and moves it to the top of the reply, as it rewrites the closing lines; a reply with no line in the form, or a line naming no option, is left as written. Without the capability the decision line is rewritten all the same, with no `over_kcal`.
   - `reserved` in the response lists the computed reservations (meal name, kcal) together with the stated ones. The response gains top-level `plan_budget` `{limit_kcal, over_kcal, reserved, choice}`, or null for any other intent, a held or refused turn, a fallback, another meal day or a missing `remaining_kcal`. A plan stays `record: none`. The adjustment call shares the route deadline. Output moderation covers the reservation labels.

Reply formatting (S30): after the closing lines of rule 5f and the plan difference of rule 3k, and before output moderation, `reply_format.shape` keeps the reply inside the subset of rule 3j: other markup is removed keeping its text (headings, links and images, code, `_`/single-`*` emphasis, quotes, rules, HTML, emoji); `*`, `+` and nested bullets become top-level `- `; `1)` becomes `1. `; the first two-column table with a separator and one to six rows stays, any other table becomes `- {item}: {grams}` lines; an unbalanced `**` or a whole bold sentence loses its bold; runs of blank lines collapse to one. A server total rewritten by rule 4a keeps its bold. Before the closing lines are rewritten, a closing line the model marked is unmarked so the server still finds it. Refusal copy, the fallback reply and the compact digest never pass through it.
5b. Legacy clients without facts receive empty memory lists. Plans return estimate null to avoid a recording card in APKs through 0.0.3; quantities/totals stay in reply.

## IN and failures

Canonical examples and base fields: [HTTP contract](../../api-contract.md#post-v1chat).

- Messages: up to 12, user/assistant roles only; digests: up to two. Text and message text: up to 2000 code points.
- Discovery: false by default, JSON boolean only; compact ignores it.
- Recipes: up to 30 `{id,name,kcal,p,c,g,key_foods}`, id `R[0-9]{1,4}` and unique; recipe_full adds 1–30 ingredients and up to 10 steps, its id in recipes; otherwise 422.
- Facts: up to 75 `{id,kind,category,key,text,slot,days_seen,last_seen}` plus optional `kcal` (0–5000), `p`, `c`, `g` (0–1000). Id matches `[PDT][0-9]{1,4}`; T iff kind temp, otherwise 422. Kinds: permanent/dynamic/temp. Categories: preference/portion/routine/equipment/liked. Key at most 40, text at most 160, slot profile id or null, days_seen nonnegative, last_seen ISO date or null. Present even empty means structured memory.
- Recent: up to 42, text at most 240, ISO date, nullable slot. Recent_days: up to 7 `{date, recorded, kcal, p, c, g, ceiling_kcal, over_slot, missing_slots}`; `recorded` a JSON boolean; slot ids from the profile, otherwise 422. Day.remaining_kcal is an integer or null, computed by the app and possibly negative.
- Clarify_rounds: 0–3 or absent. Force_estimate: false by default, ignored without rounds.
- Auto_record: false by default, effective only with rounds.
- Workout: false by default, JSON boolean only, requires rounds and auto_record, otherwise 422; compact ignores it.
- Plan_budget: false by default, JSON boolean only, requires rounds and auto_record, otherwise 422. Fit_kcal: integer 1–5000, only with plan_budget, otherwise 422. Compact ignores both.
- Temp_facts: false by default, effective only with facts. The client sends rounds and auto_record for the full v5 behavior. Older APKs get neither temp updates nor question_slot.
- Slot status: empty/eaten/skipped. Invalid input returns 422; unauthorized 401; oversized body/photo 413. Content refusals return the usual 200 shape, moderation failure 503, generation failure a 200 fallback. No 504 for the model timeout.

## Boundaries and observability

The client builds profile/day/history, applies records and stores memory; the server never reads Room. Content logging restrictions and safety correlation belong to [content policy](../../content-policy/specifications/content-policy.md) and [identity/audit](../../content-policy/specifications/identity-and-audit.md#closed-test-profile). Never log photo base64 or secrets.

For dev chat logs, `actions` lists each action's `type`, `record` and `clarify` (meal-change branch), `actions_retry` whether the question regeneration ran, `actions_dropped` the count beyond six; `effort` is the reasoning effort of the generation and `usage` its token counts (`input`, `cached`, `output`, `reasoning`); `clarify` identifies the release/hold reason and `clarify_rounds` is a number or null. `record` uses the reason labels in rule 5d or null outside v4; internal `record_intent` and `meal_day` are model enums or null. Logs also include `tone` (`seco` or `duro`), `format_stripped` (markers removed by rule 5g, or null), `plan_difference` (the kcal difference of rule 3k, or null), `plan_budget` (the returned object or null), `workout` (the returned object or null, with the capability), `adjust_retry` (whether the adjustment call ran), `meal_window` (`{window_kcal, reserved_upcoming, reserved}` of a plan of today, else null), `protein_boost` (`{source, gap_p, room_kcal, added, dropped}`: `source` `model`, `server` or `none`, `dropped` the model foods removed with their reason; null when no boost applied), `kcal_resum` (`{model_kcal, items_kcal}` when the server total replaced the model's, else null), `chosen` (the option id the plan's estimate is, or null without options), `decision_line` (`rewritten`, `off_form`, or null without options or a window), `temp_facts`, the count of T references for effective v5 or null otherwise, `question_slot`, the returned held slot or null, and `skips`, the length of the returned `skip_slots` for a client with that capability (absent otherwise). These fields contain no fact or user text. The conversation-log serializer writes these metadata. The `close` route logs `tone`, `period`, `fallback` (`false`, `error`, `policy` or `moderation`) and `close_dropped` (sentences dropped by the shaping, or null), with no photo fields.

## Functional acceptance

- A clear meal eaten today records with a known slot; true other-day meals do not. Past estimation/purchase alone does not change eating day.
- Record corrections reconstruct the meal and reuse the known/suggested slot. Day-only answers to generic questions do not record.
- Habit fallback requires two matching distinct days, or a routine fact.
- V5 labels produce reusable temp proposals; generic questions and old clients do not. Held v5 turns retain their slot; old response shapes remain compatible.
- Compaction retains stated facts and pending questions, excludes assumptions/record status, and fails closed on moderated output.
- Auth, payload/photo boundaries, fail-soft generation and fail-closed moderation remain covered by route tests.
